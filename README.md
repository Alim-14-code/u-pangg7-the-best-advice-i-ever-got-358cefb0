One sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best advice the writer ever received — an entry for make.fun's The Best Advice I Ever Got bounty ($2, top 10 paid).

## Entry

> My father told me never to borrow money to look rich — the rich don't need to prove it, and the poor can't afford the act.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) reads the line from `entry.txt` and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
